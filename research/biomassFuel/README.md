The real fuel here is **Carbon Monoxide (CO)**. It is produced by burning organic fuel or charcoal inside an airtight metal drum. Since the fuel does not have enough air (Oxygen) to turn into Carbon Dioxide (CO₂), it stops halfway through the transformation—resulting in incomplete combustion and producing Carbon Monoxide (CO). This gas is flammable and is one of the gases the engine will draw in.

However, the energy density of CO alone is relatively low. Edmundo's main innovation is implementing a water dripping system, where the water droplets get hot enough to split the $H_2O$ molecules. This process is called **Thermolysis**, yielding pure Hydrogen—an exceptional fuel.

After passing through several filters, a mixture of **Hydrogen, Carbon Monoxide, and Nitrogen** (which comes from the air and remains chemically inert) reaches the motorcycle intake hose.

Finally, the motorcycle exhaust pipe releases mainly water vapor into the environment, making it completely eco-friendly.
