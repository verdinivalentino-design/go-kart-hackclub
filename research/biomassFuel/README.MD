The real fuel here is theCarbon Monoxide (CO), and it is produced burning organic fuels or carbon but it does on a airtight metal drum. As the fuel dont have the enough air (Oxygen) to become on carbon dioxide (CO₂) it gets at the "Half" of the transformation, making an incomplete combustion, getting carbon monoxid (CO). This gas is 10 times more flamable than gasoline. This is one of the gases which the motor hast to suck.

The problem here is that the energetic density that CO has is very poor. The Edmundo's real invention is implement a dripping water sistem, where the water drops get so hot that separates the H₂O atoms. This process is called Thermolysis, and with them we get pure Hydrogen, the best fuel in the world.

After some filters, to the motorcycle intake hose gets the hydrogen, carbon monoxide and Nytrogene (Which is in the air and is chimically inerent).

Finally, through the motorcycle exhaust pipe water vapor is expelled to the enviroment, being totally friendly with the ambient.
